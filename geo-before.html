Key findings

Critical — Schema markup entirely absent
No JSON-LD of any kind on salesboom.com. This is the single most impactful gap: an AI engine that encounters a query about "Canadian enterprise CRM" or "CLOUD Act data sovereignty software" has no machine-readable entity to attach to salesboom.com. The content exists — the company facts, the founding year, the product portfolio — but none of it is marked up. Zero out of 15 points in this dimension.
Critical — No FAQ or citability structure
AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Bing Copilot) cite pages that answer questions. Salesboom.com has no FAQ section, no at-a-glance summary, no comparison tables with semantic markup. The site cannot be cited by an AI engine for specific questions — the content isn't structured as answers.
Warning — Text sizes below crawlable threshold
Body text at 11–12px is below the 16px threshold used by accessibility standards and increasingly used by AI crawlers to filter "real" content from decorative text. Small-text content may be deprioritised or ignored during extraction.
Warning — Heading hierarchy inconsistencies
Section headings jump levels (h2 → h4) in product and why-choose sections. Both screen readers and AI engines use heading structure to understand page organisation. Heading skips signal structural noise and reduce confidence in the page's information hierarchy.
Opportunity — The differentiation story is ready to be marked up
The CLOUD Act / Canadian data sovereignty narrative is genuinely differentiated and, if marked up correctly, is exactly the content that AI engines will cite for high-intent Canadian enterprise queries. All the facts are present — founding year, company location, product claims, customer numbers. None of it requires new content; it requires new markup.

About this audit

This audit applies the Northlight GEO framework — a 5-dimension weighted scoring model developed for evaluating how well a website performs with AI-powered search engines (Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search).

The five dimensions and their weights are: Crawler Accessibility (25%), Citability Signals (25%), Technical SEO (20%), Schema Markup (15%), Brand Authority (15%). Each dimension is scored on its weighted contribution to 100 total points.

The baseline score of 37/100 is an estimated score based on manual review of the salesboom.com source and rendered HTML. It is not derived from a machine crawler. The methodology is the same framework applied to the proposed salesboom.ca redesign, enabling a direct before/after comparison.
