Key findings

Critical — Citability structure is almost entirely absent (11/100)
This is the single largest drag on the composite score. AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Bing Copilot) cite pages that answer questions in a structured, extractable form. Salesboom.com has no FAQ section, no at-a-glance summary block, no semantic comparison tables, and no definition blocks for its key differentiating terms. The differentiation story exists in the content — it just isn't structured as answers an AI engine can lift and cite. This dimension scores 11/100 and contributes only 2.8 of a possible 25 weighted points.
Warning — Schema coverage is partial and incomplete (42/100)
Some structured data is present, but the schema graph is incomplete. There is no FAQPage schema (there is no FAQ content to mark up yet), no complete Organization schema with foundingDate and sameAs identifiers, and no WebSite or WebPage schema on the homepage. Partial schema means AI engines get an inconsistent entity graph — they can detect that Salesboom exists, but cannot reliably extract the company's founding year, location, or product portfolio in a machine-readable form.
Strength — Technical foundations are solid (crawler 100, technical SEO 90, brand authority 90)
Three of the five dimensions are near-perfect. All pages are crawlable, indexed, and served over HTTPS. Title tags and canonicals are correct. G2, Capterra, and Crunchbase listings are confirmed and active. This is an important context for the proposal: the site doesn't need to be rebuilt from scratch — it needs citability structure and a complete schema graph added to a foundation that already works.
Opportunity — The differentiation story only needs markup, not new content
The CLOUD Act / Canadian data sovereignty narrative is genuinely differentiated and is exactly the content AI engines will cite for high-intent Canadian enterprise queries. All the facts are present — founding year, company location, product claims, customer numbers, G2 and Capterra ratings. None of it requires new content to be written. It requires FAQ structure, an at-a-glance block, and a complete schema graph to make it extractable.

About this audit

This audit applies the Northlight GEO framework — a 5-dimension weighted scoring model developed for evaluating how well a website performs with AI-powered search engines (Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search).

The five dimensions and their weights are: Crawler Accessibility (25%), Citability Signals (25%), Technical SEO (20%), Schema Markup (15%), Brand Authority (15%). Each dimension is scored on its weighted contribution to 100 total points.

The baseline score of 66/100 is the result of a live automated audit run on September 14, 2026 using the Northlight GEO audit script (v2, Aug 17 2026 revision). The script crawled 20 pages under salesboom.com/products/, applied the 5-dimension weighted scoring model, and confirmed brand authority listings (G2, Capterra, Crunchbase) through manual verification. The methodology is identical to the framework applied to the proposed salesboom.ca redesign, enabling a direct before/after comparison.
