Most enterprise software stacks are assembled, not built. A CRM from one vendor, marketing automation from another, integration middleware from a third, AI tools from a fourth — each with its own data model, its own compliance posture, and its own support team. The Canadian Enterprise Stack is different: a single, in-house-built platform that spans the full revenue lifecycle from lead to customer to renewal, with AI infrastructure and data governance built in from the beginning.

Founded in Halifax in 2003 as Canada's first Cloud CRM vendor, Salesboom has spent over 20 years building rather than buying. The result is a platform where all pillars share one data model, one authentication layer, and one Canadian-hosted infrastructure — with no US hyperscaler in the chain, no CLOUD Act exposure, and no legacy acquisition debt to manage around.

Layer 01 · Core CRM & Revenue Operations

Revenue Intelligence & Data Foundation

The core CRM and revenue operations layer is where customer relationships are built and measured. Two pillars provide the foundation: a unified revenue platform and a master data management engine that keeps every other pillar's data clean.

01
Revenue Engine
CRM · GTM · RevOps

The core of the Canadian Enterprise Stack. Revenue Engine unifies CRM (lead, account, contact, and opportunity management), go-to-market execution (campaign management, pipeline orchestration), and revenue operations (forecasting, analytics, quota and territory management) under a single login. Every vertical edition — from real estate to pharmaceuticals to government — is built on Revenue Engine.

Configurable without code: fields, layouts, workflows, and reports adapt to your process, not the reverse.
AI-assisted lead scoring, next-best-action recommendations, and natural-language reporting are built into the platform — not bolted on as a third-party add-on.
Standalone 200+ editions API + MCP Canadian-hosted
02
Data Engine
Master Data Management

Enterprise data quality degrades at the source — duplicate records, inconsistent formatting, conflicting customer identifiers across systems. Data Engine is Salesboom's master data management (MDM) layer: it normalizes, deduplicates, and governs the customer record across every other pillar and every integrated system. Clean data is the prerequisite for reliable AI output; Data Engine provides it.

Deduplication and record-merge logic runs continuously — not as a quarterly cleanup project.
Governs the canonical customer record that AMS agents, Context Engine, and Revenue Engine all read from — one source of truth, no conflicting versions.
Standalone MDM Canadian-hosted
Layer 02 · Integration & AI Infrastructure

Agent Intelligence & System Connectivity

The second layer connects the Canadian Enterprise Stack to the rest of your technology environment and powers the AI agent infrastructure that automates work across it. These three pillars are what make the Stack useful alongside existing tools — not as a replacement for them.

03
Integration Station
Middleware OS · API · MCP · A2A

Integration Station is Salesboom's middleware operating system: the connectivity layer that allows the Canadian Enterprise Stack to exchange data and instructions with Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, Pipedrive, and most major enterprise platforms. It supports REST APIs, MCP (Model Context Protocol) server connections, and A2A (Agent-to-Agent) protocol — meaning it works with both traditional integrations and the emerging generation of AI-native connections.

Run Salesboom headless alongside an existing CRM — add Canadian sovereignty and AI infrastructure without replacing the system your team already uses.
MCP and A2A support means third-party AI agents can interact directly with your Salesboom data through standardized protocols, without a human logging in as an intermediary.
Standalone MCP A2A REST API
04
AMS — Agent Management System
A2A-Ready Agentic Infrastructure · Canada's First

AMS is Salesboom's AI agent management layer — the first published A2A-capable CRM infrastructure in Canada. AMS deploys autonomous sourcing agents that find, vet, and add leads, opportunities, employees, contractors, skills, and other agents to your CRM, measured against department-level Ideal Customer Profiles. Human-in-the-loop approval is required before any record is added — AMS proposes, your team decides.

Agents operate through A2A protocol — they communicate directly with each other and with third-party software, without a human logging into each system manually.
Canada's first CRM with published A2A capability — a measurable lead over US incumbents who are still building toward the same infrastructure.
A2A Human-in-loop Canada-first Canadian-hosted
05
Context Engine
Contextual Intelligence Layer

Context Engine is the intelligence layer that enriches every interaction with the right information at the right moment. It surfaces account history, relationship signals, deal context, and competitive intelligence automatically — before a sales call, during pipeline review, or when an agent is making a recommendation. Context Engine is what makes AI recommendations accurate rather than generic: it grounds model output in your actual customer data.

Feeds directly into AMS agent recommendations — the agent's proposed actions are grounded in real account context, not pattern-matching on training data alone.
Meeting-preparation assistants, next-best-action triggers, and contract-risk analysis all run through Context Engine.
AI-native Real-time Canadian-hosted
Layer 03 · Go-to-Market & Demand Generation

Pipeline Creation & Content Execution

The third layer covers the demand-generation and content side of the revenue lifecycle: finding prospects, managing the message, and systematizing the content that moves deals forward. Three pillars operate here, each deployable independently.

06
Demandinator
Demand Generation Engine

Demandinator is Salesboom's demand generation platform: campaign management, outbound sequencing, web-to-lead capture, and bulk-email execution, designed to fill the top of the Revenue Engine pipeline. It handles both inbound conversion (landing pages, form capture, lead routing) and outbound execution (sequence-based outreach, email automation, and campaign analytics) — connected directly to CRM so there is no hand-off gap between marketing pipeline and sales pipeline.

Campaign and sequence performance feeds directly into Revenue Engine — no manual CSV export, no attribution gap between marketing spend and closed revenue.
Standalone Outbound Inbound
07
Promptuit
Prompt Management System

Promptuit is Salesboom's prompt management system: a library and governance layer for the AI prompts that drive email drafting, proposal generation, meeting summaries, and outbound messaging. As teams scale their use of AI-generated content, prompt consistency and quality control become as important as template management was in the previous generation of CRM. Promptuit is where those prompts are built, tested, stored, and governed.

Connects to Salesboom's AI Edition features — prompts are versioned and governed centrally rather than living in individual users' chat history.
Ensures AI-generated customer communications are consistent with brand standards and compliance requirements before they are sent.
AI governance Prompt library
08
Content Relationship Management
Content Intelligence · Sales Enablement

Content Relationship Management (CRM²) closes the gap between the content team and the sales team. It tracks which content assets — case studies, proposals, battle cards, one-pagers — are used at which deal stages, by which reps, with which outcomes. The result is a feedback loop between what marketing produces and what actually moves deals: content that converts gets surfaced automatically; content that doesn't gets flagged for revision.

Content usage data feeds back into Demandinator — high-performing assets are automatically promoted in outbound sequences.
Eliminates the problem of reps using outdated or off-brand content because they can't find the current version.
Sales enablement Content analytics

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Canadian Enterprise Stack?
The Canadian Enterprise Stack is Salesboom's ten-pillar enterprise platform: Revenue Engine (CRM + GTM + RevOps), Integration Station (middleware OS), Data Engine (MDM), AMS (Agent Management System), Context Engine (contextual intelligence), Demandinator (demand generation), Promptuit (prompt management), Content Relationship Management, and the implementation services layer — Fast Track, Built to Suit, and People as a Service. Each pillar was built in-house by Salesboom's Halifax team and deploys independently or as a fully integrated system, hosted on 100% Canadian bare-metal infrastructure.
What is Agent-to-Agent (A2A) and why does it matter for CRM?
Agent-to-Agent (A2A) is a protocol that allows AI agents to communicate directly with each other and with third-party software systems — without a human logging in as an intermediary for each step. In a CRM context, A2A means a sourcing agent can identify a qualified prospect in one system, check availability in a calendar system, create a lead record in Salesboom's Revenue Engine, and notify a sales rep — all without a human manually moving data between systems. Salesboom's AMS is Canada's first CRM with published A2A capability. Human-in-the-loop approval is required before any record is added to the CRM; the agents propose, the team decides.
Can the Canadian Enterprise Stack work alongside Salesforce or HubSpot?
Yes. Integration Station is designed for exactly this. Salesboom can operate headless — as a sovereign CRM layer, AI infrastructure, or demand-generation engine — connected to your existing Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, or Pipedrive instance via API, MCP, and A2A. You keep the system your team already uses; you add Canadian data sovereignty, agentic AI infrastructure, and RevOps capability on top of it, without a forced migration or a parallel data-entry problem.
What does People as a Service include that a typical managed services contract does not?
A typical managed services contract delivers defined outputs from an anonymous service team: tickets resolved, reports produced, uptime maintained. People as a Service delivers embedded specialists — named individuals with specific skills (developer, system architect, CRM administrator, data analyst, marketing operations specialist) — who work inside your organization for the duration of the engagement, with defined accountability for outcomes rather than activities. The difference is between renting capacity and embedding a cross-functional team that owns results.
How long does a Fast Track implementation take, and what does it cover?
Fast Track implementations run 4–8 weeks from kickoff to go-live. The scope covers Revenue Engine configuration (fields, layouts, workflows, and reports tailored to your sales process), data migration from your existing CRM or spreadsheet, user training for your team, and go-live support through the first 30 days. Fast Track is the right path when requirements are clear, the data to be migrated is reasonably clean, and the team has capacity to engage during the implementation window. Complex integrations, custom development, or multi-department deployments are scoped as Built to Suit engagements instead.
