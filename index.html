What is the CLOUD Act and why does it matter for Canadian enterprise data?+

The US Clarifying Lawful Overseas Use of Data (CLOUD Act) allows US law enforcement to compel American companies to produce customer data held anywhere in the world — including on servers located in Canada. If your CRM or cloud software vendor has a US parent company, your Canadian enterprise data can be accessed by US authorities without a Canadian warrant. Salesboom is Canadian and has no US parent entity. Your data is governed exclusively by Canadian law and is not subject to CLOUD Act compulsion.

What is the difference between data residency and data sovereignty?+

Data residency means your data is physically stored in a specific country. Data sovereignty means it is governed by that country's laws and cannot be compelled by a foreign government. AWS Canada, Azure Canada, and Google Cloud Canada offer data residency — but because their parent companies are US corporations, they cannot offer true sovereignty. Salesboom offers both: Canadian servers and Canadian corporate ownership.

Can we keep our existing CRM and still use Salesboom?+

Yes. Salesboom runs headless as "A CRM for your CRM." The Revenue Engine, Integration Station, and Data Engine connect to Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, Pipedrive, and most major platforms via API, MCP, and A2A. You can add Canadian sovereignty, agentic AI infrastructure, and RevOps capability without replacing your existing system.

What is the Canadian Enterprise Stack?+

The Canadian Enterprise Stack is Salesboom's ten-pillar enterprise platform: Revenue Engine (CRM + GTM + RevOps), Integration Station (middleware OS), Data Engine (MDM), AMS (Agent Management System), Demandinator (demand generation), Promptuit (prompt management), Content Relationship Management, Context Engine, plus Fast Track, Built to Suit, and People as a Service. Each pillar deploys independently or as an integrated system, hosted on 100% Canadian bare-metal infrastructure.

What is People as a Service?+

People as a Service is Salesboom's fractional talent delivery model. Rather than selling software alone, Salesboom provides dedicated Canadian professionals — developers, system architects, marketers, data analysts, operations specialists, and CRM concierge teams — embedded in your organization for the engagement. This is not Sales as a Service; it is a full cross-functional capability.

What is A2A and how does Salesboom use it?+

Agent-to-Agent (A2A) is an integration protocol that allows AI agents to communicate directly with each other and with third-party software without a human logging in. Salesboom's AMS deploys A2A-enabled sourcing agents that find, vet, and add leads, opportunities, employees, contractors, skills, and other agents to your CRM. Salesboom is Canada's first CRM with published A2A capability.
